# Started-session toggles move to the header; the right-edge Rail serves blank screens

The Rail pinned both region toggles to the right edge of the GUI at all times (`position: fixed; top: 50%; right: 0` in the `shell.overlay` layer). In a working session the DSH shell renders its Turn Navigator — the vertical rail of turn marks that lets a long conversation jump between turns — in the rightmost ~12–40px of the center conversation column, vertically centered (`dsh-client-ui-chat`, a 28px-wide frame at `right: calc(12px - (var(--dsh-composer-side-clearance) + 16px))`, z-index 7). The two bars sit at exactly the same place at exactly the same height: the Rail's z-index 60 and `pointer-events: auto` covered the turn-mark lane and stole its clicks, and the marks rendered into the Rail's footprint. Moving the Rail vertically does not dodge a vertically centered marker band, and insetting it horizontally leaves a bar floating over conversation text.

We decided the right-edge Rail renders on screens where DSH hides the session header: the hero and a new blank session. The Turn Navigator does not exist on those screens. Once the session starts, the same two-button pair (Sidebar toggle, Panel toggle) renders in the shell's `conversation.session.header.utilities` slot, the additive, right-aligned utility row the shell ships for this control. The header is at the top of the column, and the Navigator is vertically centered, so the collision disappears by construction. Both seats build their pair from one shared `createRegionToggleButtons` factory, so handlers, glyphs, titles and disabled logic cannot drift between seats. The `shell.overlay` occupant still mounts the regions themselves (the bottom Panel and the blank-session overlay Sidebar) in every state. The Rail component keeps owning the layout effect that docks and closes the Details Column.

Consequences. A blank new session keeps the Rail, so both regions have an entry point while DSH omits the header utilities row. After the first message starts, the toggle chrome moves into the session header, VS Code title-bar style. CONTEXT.md defines the Rail as the blank-screen seat and names the started-session seat Header Toggles. Rejected alternatives: re-anchoring the Rail to a corner that the Navigator's centered band could still reach on short viewports; and insetting the Rail by the navigator lane width, which places a 22px bar over chat content and still guesses at shell-internal geometry (`--dsh-composer-side-clearance`) this repo does not own.
