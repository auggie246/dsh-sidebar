# Region toggles move to the session-header utilities row; the right-edge Rail stays hero-only

The Rail pinned both region toggles to the right edge of the GUI at all times (`position: fixed; top: 50%; right: 0` in the `shell.overlay` layer). In a working session the DSH shell renders its Turn Navigator — the vertical rail of turn marks that lets a long conversation jump between turns — in the rightmost ~12–40px of the center conversation column, vertically centered (`dsh-client-ui-chat`, a 28px-wide frame at `right: calc(12px - (var(--dsh-composer-side-clearance) + 16px))`, z-index 7). The two bars sit at exactly the same place at exactly the same height: the Rail's z-index 60 and `pointer-events: auto` covered the turn-mark lane and stole its clicks, and the marks rendered into the Rail's footprint. Moving the Rail vertically does not dodge a vertically centered marker band, and insetting it horizontally leaves a bar floating over conversation text.

We decided the right-edge Rail renders only while no session is active — the hero page, where the Turn Navigator does not exist — and while a session is active, the same two-button pair (Sidebar toggle, Panel toggle) renders in the shell's `conversation.session.header.utilities` slot, the additive, right-aligned utility row the shell ships for exactly this kind of control. The header is at the top of the column, the Navigator is vertically centered, so the collision disappears by construction. Both seats build their pair from one shared `createRegionToggleButtons` factory, so handlers, glyphs, titles and disabled logic cannot drift between seats; the `shell.overlay` occupant still mounts the regions themselves (the bottom Panel and the blank-session overlay Sidebar) in every state, and the Rail component keeps owning the layout effect that docks and closes the Details Column.

Consequences. The toggle chrome for a session lives in the session header, VS Code title-bar style; the hero keeps a single re-entry strip because no header exists there. CONTEXT.md re-defines Rail as hero-only and names the session seat Header Toggles. Rejected alternatives: re-anchoring the Rail to a corner that the Navigator's centered band could still reach on short viewports; and insetting the Rail by the navigator lane width, which places a 22px bar over chat content and still guesses at shell-internal geometry (`--dsh-composer-side-clearance`) this repo does not own.
